01THE CUP THAT STARTED A CONVERSATION

A small cup.
A big idea.

✦Not just
a container.

What if the best part of your drink was the cup you could eat after?

CUP2EAT began with a simple question and a founder who wanted everyday beverage rituals to feel more thoughtful. Varun Khera imagined a cup that could be part of the treat—not something you set aside when the good part is over.

Today, we’re exploring edible cups and more considered ways to serve. One delicious moment at a time.

OUR MISSION

Make the everyday drink break more imaginative, with edible cups made for sharing, savouring and talking about.
